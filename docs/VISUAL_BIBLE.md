# Lost & Sorted visual bible

## Identity
Premium illustrated 2.5D airport, original art only. Baggage tags and airport
departure boards carry the identity. No checklist marks, letter booster icons,
generic card-grid airport, or photorealism.

## Palette and materials
Navy #17223D, cream #FFF8EB, sky #DDEFF4, mint #75CDB2, coral #F06F6C,
yellow #F7C75A, blue #5B91E8. Buildings use warm roofs, blue glass, cream walls,
navy side faces. Matte luggage has bright edges, darker side panels and seams.
Night scenes use layered navy, mint runway lamps and warm windows.

## Geometry
2px UI strokes, 3px luggage outlines at 432x768. Detail strokes 1-2px. Soft
contact shadows: navy at 12-18% opacity, offset 0/4. Corners: tags 5px, buttons
18px, bottom sheets 28px. Buildings are silhouettes in a shared isometric
projection; stages change footprint, roof, glass and infrastructure.

## Typography
Bundled Godot fallback font, no newly downloaded font. Display 30-34, headings
22-26, body 16-18, captions 12-14. Navy on cream; cream on departure boards.
EN/UK copy is external. Text size setting scales hierarchy. Tags use bold codes.

## Families
- Luggage: roller shell, tall cabin, curved duffel with straps, backpack with
  pocket, large reinforced suitcase, instrument case with narrow neck.
- Airport: connected scene with terminal wings, approach road, tower, service
  vehicles, parked plane and runway. Seven buildings occupy logical scene
  locations. Stage 0 is worn but colorful; stage 3 has glass, lights and detail.
- Buttons: dimensional colored CTA with small press scale; icon-only tools
  have actual stroked pictograms, visible count and touch help.
- Backgrounds: illustrated airport horizon; quiet terminal floor behind puzzle.
  Campaign worlds have themed scenery and curved routes with varied nodes.
- Branding: coral suitcase + cream baggage tag + subtle plane cue. Wordmark
  LOST & SORTED in navy/cream with yellow tag accent, never a checkmark.

## Motion and sound
Press 100ms/0.96 scale, appearance 180ms/8px, availability 140ms elevation pop,
pickup 90ms + travel 200ms, dispatch glow/stamp/slide 350ms, upgrades 600ms,
reward coins/stars 500ms. Reduced motion removes translation/scale/particles.
Idle scene motion is gentle and stops with Reduced Motion. Light pickup haptic,
medium dispatch, stronger win/upgrade. All audio is short offline synthesized
percussion/chimes with separate tap/drop/match/combo/key/reveal/upgrade/coins/
star/unlock/failure/cash-out motifs.

## Store
1024x500 feature: terminal windows/runway in rear, conveyor in middle,
colorful luggage in front, logo and SORT. FLY. BUILD. in clear negative space.
Screenshots must come from actual game, framed in cream/navy travel-poster
presentation. Frames never invent features or obscure gameplay.

## Generated illustration prompt
Built-in imagegen, stylized-concept: original premium casual 2.5D airport
landscape; navy/cream/sky/mint/coral/yellow/blue; warm matte illustrated terminal,
blue glass, tower, runway, plane and service vehicle; no text/UI/logos/watermarks,
no photorealism; portrait, upper half horizon/sky, lower half quiet airport apron.
Used as atmosphere behind interactive vector buildings, never as fake UI.
