Offline game data

The core game has no account, login, backend, or cloud database. Progress, settings, scores, daily status, and optional local quality-assurance events are stored on the player's device. The game does not ask users to enter personal information.

Advertising

Advertisements are optional and disabled when the advertising integration is not configured. If the publisher enables Google AdMob, Google advertising and consent services may process device, advertising, approximate-location, diagnostic, and interaction information under Google's policies and the consent choices available to the user. Gameplay remains available when ads are unavailable or declined.

Network and sharing

The core game requires no network connection and does not send gameplay progress to the publisher. Device backup services controlled by the operating system may handle app data independently.

Children and legal review

The publisher must complete the store age/content questionnaire, audience selection, consent configuration, and jurisdiction-specific legal review before release. This repository does not claim legal certification.

Changes

If data practices change, the publisher will update this policy and its effective date before releasing the changed application.
