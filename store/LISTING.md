# Store listing — 2.1
Title: Lost & Sorted: Airport Puzzle

Short description: Sort luggage. Clear flights. Build your own airport.

Full description:

Every suitcase has somewhere to go. Choose exposed luggage, match three flight
tags and keep your seven-slot conveyor moving. Plan what you uncover: several
routes are open at once, and one extra tag can fill the belt.

Travel through 75 stages across five illustrated airport worlds. Reveal hidden
tags, find keys, clear Priority Flights and choose destinations for Transfer
Baggage. Boards are checked before play and retries keep the same layout.

Rebuild one airport from a modest regional terminal into a modern hub. Tap
connected buildings, preview their next stage and earn small permanent perks.
Original illustrated art, physical luggage and a lively conveyor bring every
dispatch to life.

Take an Airport Shift: earnings grow between flights. Cash Out safely or risk
another round for a larger multiplier. Failure affects only unbanked Shift
Earnings. Return for Today's Flight with a daily streak and best performance.

Play offline without an account. Optional ads are never required. Ukrainian
and English, reduced motion, text size, audio and haptics are included.

Release notes: New airport art and suitcase identity; global renovation with
affordable upgrades; Shift banking; interactive tutorials; polished Daily,
results and Settings; stronger difficulty evaluation; safe migration of saves.
