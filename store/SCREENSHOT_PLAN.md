# Screenshot checklist

Actual desktop captures are stored in `store/screenshots/`.

- `home.png`: logo, progress, primary modes and navigation.
- `campaign.png`: world cards, completed/locked levels and star state.
- `gameplay.png`: layered Mystery board, seven-slot tray and boosters.
- `airport.png`: renovation scene, completion percentage and stage costs.
- `daily.png`: deterministic mixed-mechanic daily board.

Before store upload, recapture at required Android phone resolution from an internal-track build, using a clean profile plus prepared states for World 5 and a win result. Keep system bars and ad test labels consistent with Play requirements; do not composite fake gameplay.
